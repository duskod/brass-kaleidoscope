**Full description** (4000 max):
A kaleidoscope you can point at the light.

Brass Kaleidoscope is not a symmetry filter. It simulates the instrument: coloured glass sits in a backlit cell with real physics, and two mirrors reflect the wedge between them around the apex. Turn the tube and the glass tumbles under gravity. No pattern ever comes back.

• Turn the phone in your hand and gravity swings round the cell, exactly like turning a real tube. Shake it to shake the glass.
• Point the back camera at a lamp or the sky and the glass blazes; point it at the floor and it goes dim. Your phone is the light meter.
• Teleidoscope: look through the camera instead of at glass. Pinch to zoom the lens.
• Fifteen glass sets, from Venetian jewel tones to sea glass and aurora, plus your own custom scheme.
• Choose the mirror angle: 60° gives six-fold symmetry, 22½° gives sixteen.
• Dry or oil-filled cell. Dry glass clatters; oil-filled drifts.
• Snapshot renders the pattern at your screen's resolution and opens the share sheet, so "Set as wallpaper" is a tap away.

Runs entirely on your phone. No account, no ads, no data collected. Camera and motion readings never leave the device.
