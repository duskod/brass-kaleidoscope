Built the way Brewster's original works: two front-surface mirrors meet at an angle, and whatever sits in the wedge between them is reflected around the apex. 60° gives six-fold symmetry, 22½° gives sixteen. The glass lives in a backlit cell at the far end and tumbles under gravity as you turn the tube, so no pattern ever comes back.
